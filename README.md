## 🧠 About KINETIX

**KINETIX** is an AI-powered rehabilitation intelligence platform designed to make physical rehabilitation more **personalized, measurable, and adaptive**.

Instead of following a fixed rehabilitation routine, KINETIX continuously analyzes exercise performance and movement patterns to provide intelligent feedback and support a **closed-loop rehabilitation journey**.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Traditional rehabilitation can be challenging because:

- Rehabilitation plans may follow a fixed routine.
- Patients may perform exercises with incorrect posture or technique.
- Progress can be difficult to measure continuously.
- Physiotherapists cannot always monitor every exercise session.
- Patients may lack immediate feedback during home rehabilitation.

### The Gap

> **Patients need continuous, intelligent feedback — not just a fixed exercise plan.**
